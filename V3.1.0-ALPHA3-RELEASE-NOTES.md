# AnchorWeight v3.1.0-alpha.3

## Verification-Aware AI Policy

This alpha connects declared AI-agent policy to provider verification evidence. Operators can now express exact rules such as verified OAI-SearchBot = allow, unverified OAI-SearchBot = deny, and verified GPTBot = deny.

Alpha.3 is additive and shadow-first. With no verification-aware rules configured, behavior is intentionally inherited from alpha.1 declaration policy. Verification never grants trust and no provider is automatically allowed or blocked solely because verification succeeded or failed.

The v3.0 frozen contract, privacy invariants, client-scoped enforcement gates, and HawkHost degraded-client-identity safety posture remain intact.
