How to read this dashboard

A lure visit means a client found the trap entrance. A valid traversal means it followed a cryptographically issued child. A Proof-of-Crawl is reached at the configured depth. In Shadow Mode, AnchorWeight records what it would quarantine without diverting traffic. In Enforce Mode, convicted clients receive inert HTTP 200 decoys instead of reaching the real origin.
